Neko Roll Privacy Policy

App owner: Ashot Manukovich. Effective date: 25.10.2026.

In short: Neko Roll does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It does not use the internet and contains no ads or analytics. All progress is stored only on your device.

Data the app collects

None. The app does not request or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, device or advertising identifiers, or any other information about the user or the device.

Data sharing

The app does not use the network and makes no network requests. No data is sent to the developer or to third parties, and no data is sold.

Ads and analytics

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that could collect data. There are no purchases for real money in the app.

What is stored on the device

So that the game remembers progress, a game data file is saved in the app's private folder on the device:

  • collected crystals, stars and best level results;
  • cat colors and outfits bought with in-game crystals;
  • the cat's fullness and the date of the last daily gift;
  • settings: volume, vibration, calm mode, graphics quality.

This data never leaves the device and contains no information about the user.

Permissions

The app requests a single permission: vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE). It is used for short haptic feedback during play and can be turned off in the settings.

Children

The game is made for children and families. Because the app collects no data at all, including children's data, it requires no registration and offers no way to communicate with other people.

How to delete data

All game data is stored only on the device. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data: Settings → Apps → Neko Roll → Storage → Clear data. Menu names may differ slightly between phones.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
